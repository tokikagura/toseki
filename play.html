Prototype implementation notes

The advanced boat rule is not included in this first browser build. A fast piece may move one or two orthogonal cells but cannot jump over terrain or another piece.

For this digital prototype, a trap is consumed after it triggers. If a two-cell fast move crosses a hidden trap on the intermediate cell, the piece stops there and triggers it.

During the CPU run, the CPU move evaluator receives only terrain, pieces, supplies and already-triggered trap information. It does not receive your hidden trap coordinates.
