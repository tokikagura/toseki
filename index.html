TOSEKI渡石

A two-player time-attack strategy board game about route choice, supplies, hidden traps, and getting your leader across a 9×9 field before your score runs away from you.

2 players 9×9 grid Hidden information Race + scoring Paper-and-stones prototype

TOSEKI is designed to be playable with simple physical components: a hand-drawn grid, stones or tokens, and small pieces of paper for secretly recording trap coordinates.

Game at a glance

Move fast. Read the map. Don’t feed your leader to a trap.

6
pieces per player

Four normal pieces and two fast pieces. Before the run begins, one of those six is designated as the leader.

3
secret traps

The defending player records three legal trap coordinates. The runner cannot see them until they are triggered.

1
leader must reach row 9

The run ends as soon as the leader reaches the farthest row of the opponent’s side.

How a round works

Two separate runs on the same battlefield.

Build the mapAgree on rivers, bridges, mountains, and 3–5 supplies.
First player deploysPlace all six pieces within the home two rows and choose a leader.
Opponent hides trapsRecord three trap coordinates in legal empty cells in the neutral zone.
Run to row 9Move one piece per turn. Gather supplies, probe for traps, and protect the leader.
Swap rolesThe other player now makes a run while the first player sets the traps.
Core rules

Small ruleset, sharp choices.

Normal piece

Moves one square in any of the eight directions. Supplies collected by a normal piece are worth 2,000 points.

Fast piece

Moves orthogonally up to two squares. Supplies collected by a fast piece are worth 1,500 points.

Leader

Any one of your six pieces can be the leader. Reaching the opponent’s farthest row ends your run. A leader that triggers a trap suffers a −3,000 point penalty.

Terrain

Rivers and mountains are impassable. Bridges allow passage across rivers. Terrain placement changes which routes are safe, fast, or easy to predict.

Supplies

Place 3–5 supply markers by mutual agreement. Supplies may be placed in your home zone or the neutral zone, never in the opponent’s home zone.

Hidden traps

Three traps are secretly placed in empty neutral-zone cells. Normal and fast pieces lose their next opportunity to move when a trap is triggered. The leader takes the score penalty above.

Trap restrictions: no traps on bridges, supplies, occupied starting cells, either player’s home zone, or the opponent’s home zone.

Scoring

Speed is valuable, but greed can still win.

Speed bonus+Supplies−Leader trap penalty

The central tension is whether to send the leader straight for the finish or spend extra turns using other pieces to collect points and reveal dangerous squares.
