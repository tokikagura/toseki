TOSEKI 渡石

Core Rules v3.5 · English reference for the current prototype

2 players9×9 gridhidden trapstime attack

1. Overview

TOSEKI is a two-player strategy game played on a 9×9 grid. Each player makes a separate timed run toward the opponent’s far edge while collecting supplies, reading the terrain, and dealing with three hidden traps set by the opponent.

2. Components

  • A 9×9 grid drawn on paper or another surface.
  • Six pieces per player, distinguishable by colour or size.
  • Paper and a pen/pencil.
  • Three small hidden notes for recording trap coordinates.

3. Board

  • Rows 1–2: the active player’s home zone.
  • Rows 3–7: neutral zone.
  • Rows 8–9: the opponent’s home zone.

Before play, both players agree on the positions of rivers, bridges, mountains and 3–5 supply markers. Rivers and mountains are impassable. Bridges allow passage across rivers.

4. Pieces

  • 4 normal pieces: move 1 square in any of the 8 directions.
  • 2 fast pieces: move orthogonally up to 2 squares.
  • 1 leader: one of the six pieces is designated as the leader before the run begins.

5. Setup and order of play

  1. Decide who makes the first run.
  2. The first runner places all six pieces in any empty cells in rows 1–2, one piece per cell, and designates a leader.
  3. The opponent secretly records three legal trap coordinates.
  4. The first player makes their complete run until the leader reaches row 9.
  5. Roles are reversed. The second player deploys, the first player sets traps, and the second player makes their run.

6. Traps

Each defending player secretly records three trap coordinates. A trap triggers when the runner enters that square.

  • Normal or fast piece: that piece cannot be moved on its next opportunity; the runner must move another piece on the next turn.
  • Leader: −3,000 points.

Traps may be placed only on empty cells in rows 3–7. They may not be placed on bridges, supplies, occupied starting cells, or either home zone.

7. Turn sequence

One turn equals one move by one piece. The active player may choose which of their pieces to move each turn. Entering a supply square collects that supply. Entering a trap square triggers it. The run ends immediately when the leader reaches row 9.

8. Supplies

  • Supply collected by a normal piece: 2,000 points.
  • Supply collected by a fast piece: 1,500 points.

Supplies may be placed only in the runner’s home zone or in the neutral zone, not in the opponent’s home zone.

10. Final score

Final score = speed bonus + supply points − leader trap penalty

Example: a 10-turn finish with one normal-piece supply and one fast-piece supply scores 7,000 + 2,000 + 1,500 = 10,500 points.

11. Determining the winner

  • After both runs, the higher qualifying score wins.
  • Normally, the second runner must exceed the first runner’s score by at least 1 point.
  • If the first runner’s leader triggered a trap, the second runner must exceed the first score by at least 3,001 points.
  • If the second runner does not meet the required margin, the first runner wins.
  • If the first runner finishes below 5,000 points, the current v3.5 prototype rule awards an automatic win to the second runner without requiring a run. This value may change after playtesting.

12. Map design guidance

  • If a river is used, two bridges are recommended for the standard game.
  • One bridge creates a much harsher choke point; three or more reduce the strategic value of traps.
  • Single mountain cells can change approach angles without turning the board into a maze.
  • Supply placement and terrain should be agreed upon before the run begins.

Prototype note: TOSEKI is still in development. Numeric balance, edge cases and optional advanced rules may change through further playtesting.
